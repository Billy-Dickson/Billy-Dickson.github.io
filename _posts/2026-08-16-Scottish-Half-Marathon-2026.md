My training for this race hasn't been too bad, I had a few health issues to contend with, that put off some of the smaller runs (thankfully only a few). With luck the remainder of the year will be better and healthier. Fingers crossed on that one. 😉

This year, I'm running with the race with "Number one niece" who I was fortunate enough to run some of the Edinburgh Marathon with last year (Well done her!!). This year, she managed to finish her half marathon debut in sub 2 hours.
